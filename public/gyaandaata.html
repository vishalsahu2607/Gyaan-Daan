Frequently Asked Questions

What is GyaanDaan?

It is a community-driven initiative that connects volunteers, learners, and donors to support education and mentorship.

Who can join?

Anyone who wants to teach, mentor, donate, or simply be part of a larger social impact movement.

What is the time commitment?

It can be as flexible as a few hours a week, depending on what suits the volunteer and the student.
